About

The Artist

Kenneth Waters is a husband and father of six who built his career on one principle: the words matter as much as the voice that carries them.

A full-time musician logging 250+ dates per year, Kenneth delivers Texas country and dancehall across honky-tonks, headline stages, and festivals throughout the region. He holds membership in both the Texas Country Music Association and the American Country Music Association.

Three charted singles on the Texas Regional Radio Report. Support slots for Pat Green, Cory Morrow, Josh Ward, and the Old 97's. Headline rooms from Dallas to Aspen, Colorado. Kenneth Waters is the real thing.
